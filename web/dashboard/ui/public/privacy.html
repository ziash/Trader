Trader Dashboard — Privacy

Updated 05 Nov 2026

Who runs it

Trader Dashboard (trader.uzlabz.com) is a private dashboard run by Zeeshan Zia for his own trading bots on demo accounts. It is not a public service.

What we receive when you sign in with Google

Your email address, whether Google has verified it, and Google's account number for you. We receive nothing else: no contacts, Gmail, Drive or other Google data.

What we keep

The above, your role and approval date, and a log of sign-ins (time, IP address, browser). We use these to decide who gets in and to spot misuse.

Where

On the site's own server at Hostinger. We do not share or sell it, or use it for advertising. One session cookie keeps you signed in. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

How long

While your access exists; the sign-in log for 12 months.

Removal or questions

zia.zeeshan@gmail.com. We delete your entry on request.
